Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026. This policy outlines how the SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society collects, uses, and safeguards information.

1. Introduction

At SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society (SAVS), accessible from slietantriksha.ac.in, we respect the privacy of our visitors and club members. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of information we collect and record, and how we utilize it.

2. Information We Collect

SAVS is a student-run academic society. We collect information through voluntary submissions on Google Forms (used for recruiting club members and registering event attendees) and basic web server analytics:

  • Registration Details: When you register for events or apply to join the club, we collect your name, registration number (student ID), branch/year, email address, phone number, and domain preferences.
  • Technical Logs: Our hosting servers (Vercel & Apache) log basic data such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamp to monitor page performance and secure the website.

3. How We Use Your Information

The personal data collected is processed solely for academic and organizational activities of the society:

  • Reviewing candidate applications for SAVS student wings.
  • Sending event invitations, schedule updates (such as meteor watch reminders), and event credentials.
  • Evaluating workshop feedback and checking system metrics to make our website more responsive.

4. Cookies and Theme Preferences

We use minimal local cookies/storage variables on your browser to preserve your layout settings (specifically your choice between Light Mode and Dark Mode theme configuration) and manage the status of the cookie consent banner. No advertisement tracking cookies are utilized.

5. Third-Party Integrations

We link to Google Forms for student submissions and external domains such as Instagram and Faculty Publications portals. This Privacy Policy applies only to our website. We recommend checking the privacy disclosures of any external link you follow.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society via our official administration email: antriksha@sliet.ac.in.
