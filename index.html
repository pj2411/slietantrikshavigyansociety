About Us

Who Are We?

The SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society is a passionate collective of students, researchers, and astronomy enthusiasts at Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology. We bridge theoretical physics with practical space technology, driving curiosity through telescopes, satellite systems, computational astrophysics, and outreach programs.

Vision & Objectives

Our primary vision is to establish SLIET as a hub for student-led space innovations, foster hands-on learning with advanced optical instrumentation, enable satellite design projects, and inspire the upcoming generation to explore cosmic phenomena.

Proven Alumni & Mentorship Legacy

Through decades of academic supervision, his scholars and mentored students have advanced into leading academic, research, and technical positions across premier universities, national institutions, and government sectors.
