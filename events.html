INAUGURATION World Space Week

SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society Inaugurated

Theme: "Inspiring Minds, Exploring Space"

Date: October 7, 2026
Venue: SLIET Longowal

Longowal, October 7: The SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society was inaugurated at the Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology (SLIET) as part of the World Space Week celebrations. The inauguration was conducted by Professor Mani Kant Paswan, Director, SLIET and Patron of the Society.

Addressing the gathering, Professor Paswan highlighted India’s achievements in space science and ISRO’s contributions. He observed that ideas and concepts found in the Vedas have inspired scientific inquiry, including research related to space science. He encouraged students to develop curiosity and a scientific outlook, and expressed the hope that the Society would provide them with opportunities to learn, participate and pursue research in this exciting field.

Professor Ravi Kant Mishra, Founder President of the Society and Professor in the Department of Mathematics, conceived the initiative in 2006. Under his guidance, more than 25 postgraduate, M.Phil. and Ph.D. students have completed their studies in astrophysics and space science. In his address, Professor Mishra said that the Society would bring together students, researchers and all those interested in space, providing them with a platform to learn, exchange ideas and engage in research.

He encouraged students from all disciplines to join the Society and take part in activities such as sky-watching, telescope demonstrations, talks, quizzes, exhibitions and school outreach programmes. Referring to the Chandrayaan-3 mission, he described its success as an inspiring example of ambition, perseverance, teamwork and continuous learning. He told students: “When students look up at the stars, they learn to look beyond limits and when they work together, they begin to reach them.”

Members of the space science team Dr. Prabhdeep Kaur, Dr. Tejinder Singh, Dr. Sudhir, Dr. Yogesh, Ms. Kanika, Dr. Himanshu, Ms. Navya, Ms. Priya, Ms. Sandhya, Ms. Ananya, Ms. Meetali, Ms. Lovleen and Mr. Siddharth—presented their research. Professor Rangan Kumar Guha, Co-Patron of the Society; Professor Surinder Singh, Dean (Research and Consultancy); Professor P. Kumar, Dean (Student Welfare); and Mr. H. Yadav, Principal of Kendriya Vidyalaya, SLIET, also attended the programme. Mr. Yadav addressed the gathering.

The SLIET community congratulates Professor Ravi Kant Mishra and his team on this initiative. The Society is expected to nurture scientific curiosity and connect students with opportunities to learn, research and innovate in space science.

OUTREACH School Level

Space Science Quiz at Kendriya Vidyalaya

Fostering Space Curiosity Among School Students

Date: October 6, 2026
Venue: Kendriya Vidyalaya, SLIET

As part of its community and school outreach activities, the SLIET Antriksha Vigyan Society successfully organised a Space Science Quiz at Kendriya Vidyalaya, SLIET.

The event witnessed enthusiastic participation from students who demonstrated their knowledge and passion for astronomy and space exploration. The quiz was designed to test their awareness of ISRO's missions, planetary science, and general astrophysics concepts.

Prizes were presented to the winners to encourage their scientific curiosity. Additionally, 24 SLIET students were recognised for their outstanding efforts in organising and conducting the outreach programme smoothly.
